🪙 What is Token Dust and Why is Your SOL Trapped?

Every time you receive an SPL token or trade on a Solana DEX, the Solana runtime creates an Associated Token Account (ATA). To keep that account open in global validator memory, Solana locks approximately 0.00203928 SOL as rent-exempt storage deposit.

Over months of trading, airdrops, and meme speculation, most active Solana wallets accumulate dozens or hundreds of obsolete accounts holding microscopic, unsellable token remnants ("dust") worth less than $0.001. Those accounts quietly lock away valuable SOL that belongs back in your wallet!
